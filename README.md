**Where People Live**

"Where People Live" is a game that challenges players to name as many real cities around the world as they can. The more cities you know, the more points you earn.
Beyond testing geographical knowledge, the game encourages curiosity about the world and reminds us that people live in countless places, from villages to megacities. Its goal is to inspire a more global perspective and a greater awareness of the diverse places people call home.

Play at: [Where People Live](https://wherepeoplelive.s3.amazonaws.com/index.html)

## Data

City data from [GeoNames](https://www.geonames.org/).
